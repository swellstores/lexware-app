Create invoices and credit notes in Lexware Office, formerly lexoffice, for your Swell orders. When an order is paid, its invoice is created in Lexware with your number range, layout and texts, and when a payment is refunded, a credit note that names the invoice.

Connect Lexware with an API key and choose drafts to review in Lexware or finalized documents. Each document is listed on its order in Swell with its number, its status in Lexware, the PDF and a link into Lexware.

- **Invoices to the cent.** Each item, shipping and discounts are on the invoice, and its total is what the customer paid. A paid order's invoice says that it's already paid, and how.
- **The right tax type.** German VAT on net or gross invoices, the destination country's VAT for EU consumers under the One-Stop-Shop, tax-free intra-community supplies to EU businesses with a VAT ID, tax-free exports, and invoices without VAT for small businesses. The app uses the tax your store charged.
- **Credit notes for refunds.** A full refund repeats the invoice's lines, and a partial refund is split over the invoice's tax rates.
- **Drafts or finalized documents.** Start with drafts and finalize them in Lexware, or create finalized documents with a PDF at once. Finalized documents can be e-mailed to your customers, in German or English, with a link to the PDF.
- **Never created twice.** Each order gets one invoice and each refund one credit note, also when a request is repeated or its answer is lost. Documents Lexware refuses are listed under Needs attention with the reason, your administrators get an e-mail, and you can retry them one by one or all at once.
- **Invoices on demand.** Create the invoice for an order from its page, or for selected orders at once, including unpaid orders and orders from before you installed the app.

Setup takes a few minutes. In Lexware, complete your company and tax settings and create an API key under Extensions → Public API, which needs the Lexware XL plan. In Swell, paste the key in the app settings, run Test connection, choose drafts or finalized documents, and turn on Create documents automatically. Orders in other currencies than EUR are skipped.
